VIVARIUM: The Reincarnation Protocol

"The Warden never watched the gate to keep mortals in. He kept vigil so the sleeping abyss below would never hear our footsteps."

VIVARIUM is an atmospheric retro roguelike action game engineered on a seamless 360-degree cylindrical wrapped planet. Walk endlessly in any direction, dive through subterranean magma shafts, scale towering cloud platforms, and take flight into orbital space dogfights to shatter the eternal cage of rebirth.

KEY FEATURES

  • 360° Cylindrical World: True horizontal wrap-around world physics — flank bosses, kite hordes around the planet circumference, and discover hidden campfire sanctuaries.
  • Vertical Biome Ascension: From scorched lava depths and ancient underground ruins up to floating sky platforms, bouncy cumulus clouds, and low-gravity orbital space arenas.
  • 9 Hero Classes: Unlock and master distinct archetypes including the Knight (Bulwark Shield), Wizard (Homing Arcane Missiles), Rogue (Smoke Bomb), Paladin, Ninja, Priest, and the secret Liberator.
  • 22 Weapons & Evolutions: Stack synergistic builds — Skyspear, Quake Stomp, Arcane Nova, Orbiting Orbs, and high-tier evolutions.
  • Meta-Progression & Sanctuary: Bank gold between runs, level up Hero Masteries, catalog 40+ beasts in the Bestiary, and track Sanctuary Synchronization.
  • Progressive Lore Decryption: Conquer runs across 4 difficulty tiers to piece together the Chronicles of the Ring World and tease the ancient apex horror: The Chrono-Leviathan.
  • Daily Runs & Streak System: New procedural seed every 24 hours with persistent daily streak flames.
  • Zero External Dependencies: Built entirely in lightweight vanilla Canvas2D + WebAudio (running smoothly in 60 FPS on desktop and mobile).
