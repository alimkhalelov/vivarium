# Brag Plan: VIVARIUM — The Reincarnation Protocol

## What is this app?
A fast-paced retro roguelike bullet-hell survivors game set on a 360-degree cylindrical wrapped planet, where the player ascends vertically from subterranean magma depths to orbital space dogfights against cosmic deities.

## The angle
"A PLANET THAT WRAPS. A BULLET HELL THAT ASCENDS."
Unlike conventional flat Vampire Survivors clones, every weapon projectile, enemy swarm, and laser curves around the planet's gravitational horizon. Survival demands not just dodging on a flat 2D plane, but conquering a multi-tier vertical world from underground lava caverns, ancient ruins, and cloud kingdoms to orbital space.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
**The 5-Second Money Shot (xkoster Rule #1):**
Immediate high-stakes orbital dogfight in deep space.
The hero flies with celestial wings above the glowing curvature of the blue planet, unleashing a blinding barrage of homing Arcane Missiles and luminous Skyspears while executing micro-dodges through dense spiraling bullet-hell rings of "THE HIGHEST BEING".
Instant takeaway within 3 seconds: "Vampire Survivors meets Super Mario Galaxy with intense arcade bullet-hell combat."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Wrapped World Physics & Horde Slicing (4.0s - 8.0s)**:
  Sprinting across the looping planetary surface, kiting massive mob sieges into gravitational choke-points, and triggering devastating screen-clearing auto-attacks.
- **Deep Caverns & Boss Slaying (8.0s - 12.0s)**:
  Plunging into dark subterranean dungeons, defeating the fiery Wing Guardian, opening legendary treasure chests, and stacking game-breaking synergy cards.
- **Vertical Ascent: Hell to the Clouds (12.0s - 16.5s)**:
  Racing up the rising molten lava shaft, leaping between sinking platforms, and battling celestial sentinels high in the cloud kingdom.
- **The Orbital Climax & True Cosmic God (16.5s - 19.0s)**:
  Breaking through the atmosphere into zero-gravity planetary orbit for the final cosmic showdown.

## Outro / punchline (19.0s - 21.5s)

- Role: Dense rhythmic layer with cinematic synthwave/electro drive and punchy arcade impact SFX.
- Music: Bundled high-energy electronic track (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` or dynamic electronic synth theme).
- Music treatment: Kicks in with a sharp drop at 0.0s for the Money Shot, drives relentless momentum through the middle gameplay beats, builds into a rise at 17s, and hits a heavy final sub-bass boom on the Steam Wishlist card at 19.5s.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Orbital Money Shot — 4.5s (0.0s - 4.5s)
- **Visual**: In deep space, orbiting above the vivid blue curvature of the planet. Player dodges massive purple bullet rings from "THE HIGHEST BEING" while blazing orange missiles arc around the planetary horizon.
- **Overlay Text**: "A PLANET THAT LOOPS." (0.6s) → "A BULLET HELL IN ORBIT." (2.2s)
- **Audio intent**: Explosive hook! Immediate driving electronic beat and laser impacts.
- **Transition mood**: Hard slam cut on heavy beat → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Looping Surface & Horde Siege — 4.0s (4.5s - 8.5s)
- **Visual**: Daylight Overworld & Ancient Ruins. Player leaps across green hills and ancient columns while dozens of flying monsters swarm in a 20-second survival siege. Screen fills with damage numbers, gem bursts, and slicing sword arcs.
- **Overlay Text**: "KITE THE CYLINDER." (5.0s) → "STACK THE SYNERGIES." (6.8s)
- **Audio intent**: High tempo, rapid hit sounds, XP gem pickup chimes.
- **Transition mood**: Rapid swipe cut → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Dungeon Depths & Boss Battles — 4.0s (8.5s - 12.5s)
- **Visual**: Underground caverns. Wing Guardian unleashes glowing fireball rings. Chest opens with golden flash, level-up cards flash, and weapons evolve into Arcane Nova.
- **Overlay Text**: "DESCEND INTO THE DEPTHS." (9.0s) → "SLAY COSMIC SENTINELS." (10.8s)
- **Audio intent**: Deep bass, chest chime, heavy explosion SFX.
- **Transition mood**: Sinking transition into fire → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Vertical Ascension: Hell to Heaven — 4.0s (12.5s - 16.5s)
- **Visual**: Frantic escape up the rising lava shaft with red-hot magma below, jumping across crumbling platforms, cutting directly into celestial flight through heavenly clouds against The Archangel.
