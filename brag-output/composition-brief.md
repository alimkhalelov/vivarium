## Creative Direction (xkoster 5 Laws)
1. **The 5-Second Money Shot**: Kick off immediately at 0.0s with the highest-stakes orbital space fight against the cosmic eye of God. Zero walking, zero waiting.
2. **Alive & Clear Gameplay Without Water**: High-density action, cutting directly between core progression mechanics and vertical biomes.
3. **Expressive Light, Contrast, & Aesthetics**: Saturated retro pixel art, glowing projectile trails, neon shaders, deep cosmic void contrast.
4. **Zero Cheapness or False Pathos**: Tight audio synchronization with impactful electronic beats and crisp sound effects.
5. **Idea Validation**: Instantly shows the core novelty—a roguelike survivor where the world loops and you ascend from hell to space.
