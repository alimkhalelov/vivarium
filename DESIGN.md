# DESIGN.md - Planet Survivors Aesthetics

## Visual Identity & Philosophy
- **Style**: Retro 16-bit Wrapped-World Roguelike Canvas2D.
- **Palette**: Dark cosmic void (#05030a, #0c0a14), radiant gold (#ffd040), neon exp cyan (#4ac8ff), danger crimson (#ff4050), verdant nature (#4a8a30, #68b048).
- **HUD & UI**: Clean, uncluttered, anti-slop HUD. Translucent dark glass panels (`rgba(14, 10, 24, 0.6)`), recessed slot bevels, crisp 5x7 bitmap typography.
- **Animation & Motion**: Fluid character walk bobbing and squash-stretch jumps, soft volumetric cloud drifting, particles with additive blending and radial glow.
- **Feedback**: Dynamic center-to-top floating banners for quest milestones and day breaks, responsive card hover elevations where content and frames transform in tandem.
